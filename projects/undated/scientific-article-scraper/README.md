**Institution:** University of Ghardaia — Department of Mathematics and Computer Science  
**Period:** Academic coursework · date not explicitly confirmed  
**Category:** Projects / academic coursework

## Problem addressed
Collect a small set of scientific article attributes (title, authors, publication date, DOI, abstract) to a CSV.

## Approach
Java with Jsoup, HTML selection and CSV export.

## Data and prerequisites
Network access and website selectors are needed; the site may change.
